# Customer interview workbook — Little Joys

**Research status:** No interviews have been conducted yet. Do not submit this workbook as completed research. The Flavour Finder app is only a desk-research prototype until conversations validate the underlying problem.

## Interview intent
Understand how Little Joys customers discover, choose, try, and repurchase products. Current desk research suggests NutriMix flavour choice could be worth exploring, but do not lead participants toward this idea; their experience may point somewhere else.

## Consent
"I'm a student working on the Mosaic Fellowship Builder Challenge. I'd like to ask about your experience with Little Joys and take notes. Do you agree to share your feedback for this application? You can skip any question."

Record a clear yes/no and date.

## Neutral interview guide (10–15 minutes)
1. Which Little Joys products have you personally used or purchased? When was the most recent time?
2. What was happening that led you to look for or buy it?
3. How did you decide which product or flavour to try? What else did you consider?
4. Tell me about the first time you tried it. What happened before, during, and after?
5. If you tried more than one flavour or product, how did you decide what to try next?
6. Was anything wasted, left unused, hard to prepare, or difficult to reorder? Walk me through a specific example.
7. What was the most satisfying part of the experience? What was most frustrating or uncertain?
8. Did you ask anyone for help or information? How did that go?
9. What did you do the last time your household did not like, finish, or want to continue a product?
10. If you could improve one step—from discovery through repeat purchase—which would you choose? Why?
11. What did I miss that would help me understand your experience?

Use neutral probes: “What happened next?”, “Can you tell me about a specific time?”, “Why did you choose that?”, “What did you do then?” Do not pitch the prototype or ask for sensitive health details.
