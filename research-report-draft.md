The brand targets parents of children aged roughly 2–12 who are looking for science-backed, tasty nutritional supplements.

The NutriMix range includes a **three-flavour trial pack** designed for families who are unsure which flavour their child will prefer.

## 9. How the App Solves It

**App name:** Little Joys Flavour Finder  
**Live URL:** [Your deployed URL]

**What the app does:**  
The Flavour Finder is a household tasting journal. A family opens the app after trying a NutriMix flavour, records their enjoyment rating (1–5), how the powder mixed, and whether they would choose it again. They can optionally add a short note. After a few entries, the app shows a simple summary of which flavour has the highest average rating — based entirely on the family's own saved notes, not population data.

**How it closes the gap identified in research:**

| Research finding | App feature that addresses it |
|---|---|
| [e.g., Families forget which flavours were tried and how they went] | Persistent tasting history, stored locally |
| [e.g., Parents want a nudge toward the "safer" choice] | Preference summary showing top-rated flavour from own notes |
| [e.g., Mixing experience varies and matters to families] | Dedicated texture field — "mixed smoothly / a few lumps / hard to mix" |
| [e.g., Families want something private, not another account] | No login, no account, no data leaves the device |

**What the app deliberately does not do:**  
It does not make health claims, recommend flavours based on population data, store any personal information, or pressurise families with streaks or scores. It is clearly labelled as an independent student concept and not an official Little Joys product.
